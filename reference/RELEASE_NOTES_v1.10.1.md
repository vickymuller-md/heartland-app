# HEARTLAND App v1.10.1

## Operational clarification release

This patch addresses remaining inconsistencies across the implementation tables, public guide and workflow explanations. It does not change risk-score calculations, drug doses, alert thresholds, referral criteria, database grants, memberships or operational activation flags.

- Resource tiers describe delivery capacity, not lower clinical goals or longer clinically indicated deadlines. The tier questionnaire describes a self-reported resource profile, not verified readiness. Existing quality percentages are explicitly historical planning examples, not validated benchmarks or individualized care targets.
- Community, ambulatory and remote pharmacists can participate at any tier when available and appropriately authorized. Complete reconciliation, medication understanding and access assessment remain relevant at every tier; indicated financial assistance does not wait for an upgrade.
- Trained recognition and escalation, qualified assessment, and authorized medication/disposition decisions are distinguished. Referral explanations separate planned consultation, urgent/emergency assessment and advanced-HF/inpatient context; the current owner retains responsibility until accepted transfer.
- A public four-step local-readiness guide links a sanitized training pack with 12 synthetic scenarios, role/coverage worksheets, paper records and respectful-escalation expectations. Preparing materials does not establish that staff have been trained.
- The home, printable tier output, quality-metric explanations and versioned pocket cards reflect these boundaries. The downloadable pack has one exact public route; adjacent resources and authenticated workflows remain protected.
- Public guidance no longer promises offline clinical writes, continuous monitoring or actual notification delivery. The 58 previously released synthetic recordings are unchanged. Automated checks are not human listening or clinical validation.

## Verification and continuing boundaries

Release verification covers types, lint, unit/integration tests, production build, recording identities and desktop/mobile browser behavior. Exact execution receipts are maintained with the release; skipped/TODO tests are not represented as passing validation.

Separate ambulatory entry, prospective patient evaluation and institution-specific staffing, coverage, clinical policies and operational verification remain outside this patch. Scheduled recovery and external transport remain disabled. No independent review, new clinician signature, institutional adoption, patient outcome or patient-use authorization is claimed. Earlier source archives and Toolkit V3.4 remain preserved; runtime and archive citations are identified separately until a successor archive is published.
